Press → pause → text

Try the same decoder by hand. Hold the key with a pointer, or focus it and hold Space. Short press: dot. Long press: dash.

Try ... for S, --- for O, or .- for A.
Decoded text Current marks

One unit = 150 ms. The transmitter uses a 150 ms dot and a 450 ms dash. The receiver treats presses up to 300 ms as dots.

Within a letter: pause briefly. After a letter: pause at least 450 ms. Between words: pause at least 1,050 ms.
